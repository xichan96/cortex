- Evaluates a mathematical expression and returns the result.
- A powerful calculator that supports a wide range of operations.

Usage notes:
  - Basic operators: `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`
  - Advanced operators: `^` (power), `√` or `sqrt` (square root), `%` (modulo), `!` (factorial).
  - Functions: `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `ln`, `log`, `exp`, `abs`, `floor`, `ceil`, `round`.
  - Trigonometric functions can operate in `degrees` or `radians` (default).
  - Set `"use_degrees": true` to enable degree mode.
